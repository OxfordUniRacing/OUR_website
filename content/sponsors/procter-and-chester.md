Procter & Chester (Measurements) Ltd (PCM) is a family business designing and manufacturing market-leading load cells, load pins and torque transducers. PCM also provides specialist strain gauging, bespoke design, hire and repair, and runs a force and torque calibration laboratory accredited by UKAS to ISO/IEC 17025.
