Procter & Chester (Measurements) Ltd (PCM) is a third-generation family business that has specialised in load measurement since 1983, designing and manufacturing market-leading load cells, load pins and torque transducers. PCM also provides specialist strain gauging, bespoke design, hire and repair, and runs a force and torque calibration laboratory accredited by UKAS to ISO/IEC 17025. Engineers worldwide, in demanding sectors such as motorsport, aerospace, defence and nuclear, rely on PCM for precision, traceability and rapid turnaround, with solutions built to stand the test of time.
